Cluck Crossing

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Cluck Crossing does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the game saves

To remember your progress, the game saves a few settings only on your own device:

  • The levels you have unlocked and the stars you have earned
  • Your corn total, the hens you own, the hen you are using, and your power-ups
  • Your best endless-mode distance and whether sound is on

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone
  • No network connection is needed to play

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
